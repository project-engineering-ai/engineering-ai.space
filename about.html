About Engineering AI

Engineering AI is a specialist consultancy applying artificial intelligence to transport infrastructure and fleets. We design, deploy and operate AI systems on top of existing cameras, sensors and control equipment.

What we work on

  • Adaptive intersections & corridor optimization
  • Computer vision and multi-camera safety analytics
  • Predictive maintenance for buses, trams and rolling stock
  • Private, on-premise AI stacks for operators
  • Integration with SCADA, C-ITS and existing IoT

Approach

Measured pilots first — use cases that deliver live reduction in delay, collisions or OPEX within 3–6 months. Then scale horizontally with open tooling and strict, versioned models.
