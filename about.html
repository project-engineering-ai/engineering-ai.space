About Engineering AI

Engineering AI is a specialist consultancy applying artificial intelligence to transport infrastructure and fleets. We design, deploy and operate AI systems that work with existing cameras, sensors and control equipment.

What we do

  • Adaptive intersections and corridor optimization
  • Computer vision and multi-camera safety analytics
  • Predictive maintenance on buses, trams and rail rolling stock
  • Private, on-premise AI stacks for operators
  • Integration with SCADA, C-ITS and existing IoT

Approach

We start with measured pilots, no deck-sitting. Use-cases that deliver a live reduction in delay, collisions or OPEX within 3–6 months. Then scale horizontally with open tooling and strict versioned models.
