AI for Transport Systems

We put artificial intelligence to work on the road, rail and fleet. Our team builds adaptive traffic lights, computer-vision safety analytics, predictive fleet maintenance, and MLOps pipelines designed for transport operators.

Services

  • Video Analytics & Safety — incident detection, pedestrian risk scoring, near-miss analytics from existing CCTV.
  • Predictive Fleet Maintenance — telemetry-driven models that flag component failures before downtime.
