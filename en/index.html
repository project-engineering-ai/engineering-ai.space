Artificial Intelligence for Robots and Autonomous Transport Systems

We build autonomy where it pays off fastest: in warehouses, factories and on the last mile. Robots see, plan, drive and work in swarms — we make sure this is reliable and safe around people.

What we do

  • Turnkey autonomy: machine vision and lidar, predictive route planning, precise motion control in live warehouse and factory environments
  • Robotic platforms: last-mile delivery robots, autonomous warehouse carts (AMR/AGV), transport modules for intralogistics
  • Swarm dispatching: a single control center for a transport system of hundreds of robots — task assignment, flow optimization, real-time collision-free traffic
  • V2X and smart building: we connect robots to the facility ecosystem — automatic gates, freight elevators, conveyors, WMS/WCS/ERP systems
  • Safety and validation (SOTIF): digital twins and simulation, scenario testing, functional safety standards for collaborative operation with humans
  • Secure AI lifecycle: model fine-tuning on field data, dataset management, safe OTA updates of firmware and neural networks across the whole fleet

Where to start

Tell us where you want to deploy robots — a warehouse, a production line, a distribution center or last-mile delivery. We reply within one business day.
